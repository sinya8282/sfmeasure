\subsection{Probabilistic independence of star-free and group languages}

The first consequence is the following ``probabilistic independence'' of star-free and group langauges, which emphasises orthogonality of these two language classes more formally.
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:ind}
For each star-free language \(L\) and group langauge \(K\),
we have 
\[
 \cd(L \cap K) = \cd(L) \times \cd(K).
\]
\end{theorem}
The probabilistic independence of generalised definite languages and group languages can be more easliy proved. We use it as a lemma for proving Theorem~\ref{thm:ind}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:ind}
For each generalised definite language \(L\) and group langauge \(K\),
we have 
\[
 \cd(L \cap K) = \cd(L) \times \cd(K).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let \(L = u A^* v\) be a generalised definite language, and \(K\) be a group language such that \(K = \eta^{-1}(S)\) for some finite group \(G\), its subset \(S\) and \(\eta: A^* \rightarrow G\) be a surjective morphism.
Observe that \(L \cap K = u (u^{-1} K v^{-1}) v\) holds.
Because we have \(u^{-1} K v^{-1} = u^{-1} \eta^{-1}(S) v^{-1} 
= \eta^{-1}( \eta(u)^{-1} S \eta(v)^{-1})\),
we can conclude that 
\(\cd(L \cap K) = \cd(u(\eta^{-1}( \eta(u)^{-1} S \eta(v)^{-1})) v) =
\card{A}^{-|uv|} \times \cd(\eta^{-1}( \eta(u)^{-1} S \eta(v)^{-1}))
= \cd(L) \times \cd(K)
\)
by Lemma~\ref{lem:density} and Lemma~\ref{lem:groupdensity} since 
\(\card{S} = \card{\eta(u)^{-1} S \eta(v)^{-1}}\).
\end{proof}
\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:ind}]
Let \(L\) be a star-free language and \(K\) be a group language.
We show that \(\cd(L \cap K) \geq \cd(L) \times \cd(K)\) holds.
By Theorem~\ref{thm:sf}, there is a convergent sequence \(M_n = (\bigcup_{(u, v) \in F_n} u A^* v)_{n \geq 0}\) of generalised definite languages, where \(F_n \subseteq A^* \times A^*\) is finite for each \(n\), into \(L\) from inner.
Without loss of generality, we can assume that \(uA^*v \cap u'A^*v = \emptyset\) for each different pairs \((u,v), (u',v') \in F\).
Then we have
\[
 \cd(L \cap K) \geq \cd\left(\bigcup_{(u,v) \in F_n} u A^* v \cap K\right)
 = \sum_{(u,v) \in F_n} \cd(u A^* v \cap K)
 = \cd(M_n) \times \cd(K)
\]
by Lemma~\ref{lem:ind} where \(\cd(M_n)\) tends to \(\cd(L)\) as \(n\) tends to infinity.
Hence \(\cd(L \cap K) \geq \cd(L) \times \cd(K)\).
By the same argument, the inequality
\(\cd(L \cap K) \leq \cd(L) \times \cd(K)\) can be show by using a 
convergent sequence of generalised definite languages into \(L\) from outer.
\end{proof}
