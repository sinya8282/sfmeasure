\section{Measure-Theoretic Decomposition Theorem of Regular Languages}
In this section, we give a proof of the second main theorem.
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:dec}
Every regular language is \(\bool(\sf \cup \group))\)-measurable,
\\ \ie, \(\reg = \rext(\bool(\sf \cup \group))\).
\end{theorem}
